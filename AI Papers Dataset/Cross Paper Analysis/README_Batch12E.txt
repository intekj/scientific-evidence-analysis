BATCH 12E — PILOT RELATION ANNOTATION QUEUE

This package contains 30 candidate cross-paper claim pairs selected from the existing Batch 12 comparison clusters.

CRITICAL:
- These are candidate pairs only. No support/contradiction labels have been assigned.
- Every row is marked CANDIDATE_ONLY_NOT_VALIDATED.
- Annotators must open the original PDFs and verify the exact evidence.
- The focal claim is the first claim; the comparison claim is the second.
- Use Batch 12D methodology and allowed labels.
- Do not mark CONTRADICTS unless the same construct and sufficiently comparable conditions are established.
- Mark missing metadata UNKNOWN; never infer it.
- Two annotators should label independently before adjudication.
